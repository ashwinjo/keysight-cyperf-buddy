# Phase 4.1: Sales Funnel - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-02-23
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Capture customer interest and contact information from CVE tracker users. When users discover a testable CVE ("Let's Discuss") or search for a non-testable CVE ("Request Feature"), collect their contact info (name, company, email) and send an automated email to ashwin.joshi@keysight.com with contextual details. Goal: Build a lead pipeline to understand customer demand for CVE testability and feature requests.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Button Placement & Visibility

- **"Let's Discuss" button** — Appears only in CVE detail/expanded view (not in result rows)
- **"Request Feature" button** — Appears when CVE is found but testability status is "No" (not testable)
- **Confirmation before form** — Yes, small confirmation dialog: "You're about to provide contact info. Continue?"
- **Button styling** — Claude's discretion on prominence vs subtlety

### Contact Form UX

- **Form presentation** — Sidebar panel (slides in from right, non-blocking)
- **Fields required** — Only 4 fields: first name, last name, company, email
- **Post-submission** — Show success message + email preview (user sees what was sent to ashwin.joshi@keysight.com)
- **Data persistence** — Claude's discretion (localStorage or stateless)

### Email Content & Generation

- **Email body includes** — CVE ID + details, customer's company, reason/context ("Let's Discuss" vs "Request Feature"), testability status
- **User editing** — No, email is auto-generated and read-only. User previews before sending, but cannot modify body
- **Subject line** — Auto-generated based on context:
  - "Let's Discuss": `Customer Interest: [CVE-ID] Discussion`
  - "Request Feature": `Feature Request: [CVE-ID]`
- **Email recipient** — ashwin.joshi@keysight.com (hardcoded for now)

### Authentication & Email Sending

- **Auth method** — Simple hardcoded credentials (single username/password for all email sends)
- **Session persistence** — Claude's discretion (remember login or require re-auth each time)
- **Error handling** — Claude's discretion (retry strategy, error messaging)
- **Sending mode** — Asynchronous/background (user sees "Email queued" immediately, sent in background)

### Claude's Discretion

- Button visual prominence (prominent vs subtle)
- Form data persistence (localStorage vs stateless)
- Session persistence after authentication
- Error handling and retry logic for failed sends
- Exact email template formatting and wording

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- "Request Feature" appears specifically when "Can Cyperf Test this ?" = No (not when CVE doesn't exist at all)
- Email preview should show user exactly what email will be sent to Ashwin, including all customer details
- Two paths must be clear to users: "Let's Discuss" (we can test it) vs "Request Feature" (we can't test it, but customer wants it)

</specifics>

- Notification to customer when Ashwin follows up — future phase
- Configurable email recipient in admin panel — Phase 5 or later
- Analytics on lead source (which CVEs drive most interest) — future phase

</deferred>
